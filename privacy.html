Privacy Policy

The app signs in only to the channel owner's own accounts. It stores the access tokens (encrypted) and the view counts of our own posts, so it can publish and show results.

It does not collect, sell or share data about other people. To remove the app's access, revoke it in your TikTok, YouTube or Instagram settings.
